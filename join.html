The Flying

Long-distance flying is strongly supported. There are no daily minimums, so taking the Arrow away for a week is normal rather than exceptional; club pilots regularly fly to Northern California, Nebraska, Kansas and Michigan. Trips into Mexico are permitted.

Many of our members also fly charitable missions for Pilots N Paws and Flights for Life, and the club actively encourages it.
