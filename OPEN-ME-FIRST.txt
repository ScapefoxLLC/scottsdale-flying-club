Scottsdale Flying Club — draft website
======================================

TO VIEW IT
----------
Unzip this folder somewhere, then double-click:

    index.html

It opens in your web browser. Everything works offline — click the menu
across the top to move between pages, and click any photograph on the
Photographs page to see it full size.

There is nothing to install and no internet connection required, apart
from the map on the Contact page.


WHAT TO LOOK AT
---------------
Home          Overview of the club
About         The airplane, the panel, the field, how the club runs
Join          Costs, experience required, checkout
Photographs   The photo gallery
Video         Empty for now — waiting on footage
Contact       Enquiry form and directions

"For Members" in the menu is a link out to AircraftClubs, the scheduling
system, so it will leave the site.


NOTES
-----
- The contact form is live and really does send email, so please don't
  submit it as a test.
- The Video page is deliberately empty.
- This is a draft. Comments on wording, missing information and anything
