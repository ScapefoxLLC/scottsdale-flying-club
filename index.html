Scottsdale Flying Club

Flying club with a Piper Arrow III based at Phoenix Deer Valley Airport.

Welcome

We are a non-profit flying club at Phoenix Deer Valley Airport, sharing a 2004 Piper Arrow III with a freshly overhauled engine and a well-equipped IFR panel.

The Arrow trues out at up to 135 knots on 8.5 gallons an hour, with 72 gallons of usable fuel. Long trips are encouraged rather than merely tolerated — club pilots regularly fly to Northern California, Nebraska, Kansas and Michigan, and trips into Mexico are permitted. Some of those long legs are Pilots N Paws animal rescue missions, which can run a very long way indeed. Closer to home, our pilots fly Flights for Life missions within Arizona.

At $120 per hour dry, we believe the Arrow is the most affordable complex airplane available at any flying club in Arizona — and our $300 join fee is the lowest as well.

We are a small and friendly club, and members are expected to take part in running it. Our hangar sits on the quiet north side of Deer Valley, where there are no queues to take off or land and the self-serve fuel is a short taxi away.

  • Home field — Phoenix Deer Valley Airport (KDVT)
  • Aircraft — 2004 Piper Arrow III, N414WM — 200 hp, IFR equipped
  • Cruise — up to 135 knots at 8.5 gallons per hour
  • Fuel — 72 gallons usable; 50 to the tabs
  • Rate — $120 per hour, dry, on Hobbs time
  • Membership — capped at 15 pilots; currently full, with a short waiting list
  • Scheduling — Online through Flight Circle
